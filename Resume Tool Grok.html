6-Dimension Scoring

Every dimension that hiring managers & ATS care about

Transparent scores with concrete evidence — not vague “looks good” comments.

👁

Clarity

Scannability, hierarchy, white space, and whether a recruiter can grasp your value in 6 seconds.

💥

Impact

Strong verbs, quantification, outcomes over duties. Did you move a metric or just “responsible for”?

🎯

Relevance

Alignment to target role and industry. Removes noise and elevates the experience that matters.

🤖

ATS

Keyword density, standard section headers, clean formatting, and parseability for applicant tracking systems.

📖

Story

Career narrative, progression, and how your summary + bullets tell a coherent professional story.

✍️

Language

Tone, tense consistency, grammar, jargon balance, and elimination of weak or cliché phrasing.

Best-Practice Principles

Built on what actually works

The skill applies proven resume standards automatically.

⚡

Strong Action Verbs

Led, Drove, Built, Optimized — never “Helped with” or “Responsible for”.

📊

Quantification

Metrics, percentages, dollar amounts, and scale wherever possible.

🛡️

ATS-Safe Formatting

Standard headers, no tables/columns, clean text that parsers can read.

📏

Length Guidelines

1 page for early career, 2 pages max for senior — ruthlessly prioritized.

🔑

Keyword Strategy

Natural keyword placement from the job description without stuffing.

🧭

Clear Hierarchy

Most relevant experience first, consistent date formats, scannable structure.
